Evidence: both language facts files, both sheets per language and both cover images in the recipe output review folder. Contact sheets support visual observations; no audience analytics or listening evidence was supplied.

## English

SCORE: 5.5/10
VERDICT: The home kitchen feels credible, but the opening and price presentation weaken the invitation to cook along.
WHAT WORKS:
- Real hands, familiar equipment and visible prep feel approachable.
- The noodle lift and finished plate provide a satisfying payoff.
- Short, high-contrast captions generally work muted.
PROBLEMS:
1. At 0–2 s a polished AI still precedes any real cooking; the cover's €9 badge does not identify the figure as an estimate.
2. At 20 s the prawn caption sits over sauce preparation; at 22 s it sits over pork. Picture and instruction disagree.
3. At 23–26 s “secreto ibérico” may leave English viewers unsure which meat they need.
4. At 38–41 s the ingredient bill and tiny estimate note compete for a three-second reading window; the price badge on the cover sits in the bottom interface area.
HOOK SUGGESTION: Open with the real noodle lift from raw.mp4 at approximately 72 s for 1–2 s, then retain the required two-second AI cover.
FIXES (each starts with its lever in brackets):
1. [shots] Add that moving noodle-lift hook before the cover.
2. [captions] Put each instruction on its actual action; label the griddle shot “Sear the Iberian pork” and remove the prawn instruction from sauce/pork frames.
3. [cover] Move the cover price badge above the bottom 20% and label it “Estimated per serving”.
4. [price] Simplify the short end card to estimated €18 total / serves 2 / €9 per serving; put the itemised bill and “amounts used; whole packs cost more” in the short description.

## Spanish

SCORE: 5.5/10
VERDICT: El entorno doméstico resulta cercano, pero los desfases de los rótulos y la letra pequeña restan confianza.
WHAT WORKS:
- “Secreto ibérico” y “gambones” encajan con el público de España.
- Las manos y los utensilios cotidianos hacen la receta reconocible.
- El acabado de los fideos ofrece una recompensa visual clara.
PROBLEMS:
1. En 0–2 s falta movimiento real; la portada presenta €9 sin indicar que es una estimación.
2. En 20–22 s “Sella esos gambones” acompaña azúcar/salsa y carne, no el sellado de gambones.
3. En 32–35 s “Levántalo. ¡Mira eso!” suena traducido y no ayuda a seguir la receta.
4. En 38–41 s la lista y la nota de estimación son demasiado pequeñas para leerlas cómodamente; el precio de portada queda muy abajo.
HOOK SUGGESTION: Empezar con los fideos levantándose en raw.mp4, aproximadamente 72 s, durante 1–2 s y después mostrar la portada obligatoria.
FIXES (each starts with its lever in brackets):
1. [shots] Añadir ese gancho de fideos en movimiento antes de la portada.
2. [captions] Ajustar los rótulos a cada acción y cambiar “Levántalo. ¡Mira eso!” por “Y a la mesa”.
3. [cover] Subir la insignia por encima del 20% inferior y escribir “Estimación por ración”, con formato “9,00 €”.
4. [price] Reservar la tarjeta final para “Total estimado 18,00 € / para 2 / 9,00 € por ración”; llevar el desglose y “cantidades usadas; comprar envases enteros cuesta más” a la descripción del short.
