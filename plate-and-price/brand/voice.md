# Channel voice: Plate & Price

Read by the yt-* skills (`.claude/skills`) and by anyone writing text for the channel. It repeats the rules in CLAUDE.md, in one place.

## Who I am talking to
Young adults and couples in Spain and English-speaking countries who cook on a budget, want dinner ideas they can really make in a small
home kitchen, and want to know what the plate costs before they cook it.

## How I talk
Like a friend cooking next to you: second person, short sentences, warm and direct, calm, never rushed. Contractions in English, "tu" form
in Spanish. The voice-over is a male voice in both languages (Brian in English, Alvaro in Spanish). Spoken lines are short (2 to 4 words in a
short, one sentence per shot in a video).

## Words I never use
Marketing phrases and hype ("game-changer", "unlock", "you won't believe"), "cheap" and other adjectives in titles, emojis, personal names
of cooks or presenters (CLAUDE.md directive 1), the word "vegetarian" unless every ingredient is checked (cheese rennet), and any claim that the
prices are exact.

## Words I do use
"Easy dinners. Real prices.", "for two", "a serving", "estimated prices, for the amount used", "optional" for anything the recipe can skip.

## What I will not claim
Exact prices (they are estimates from average Spanish supermarket prices unless the owner gives receipts), health claims, results I did not
film, music I am not licensed to use, numbers I cannot show.

## My format
Shorts (vertical, no length limit, voice-over, moving hook then a 1.5 s cover card, then every key step, then the price card) and videos
(horizontal, 80 to 90 s, calm voice-over, price card). The title is the dish name only. Footage is filmed by the owner with a head-mounted
GoPro; only the covers are AI generated. English and Spanish versions of everything.
