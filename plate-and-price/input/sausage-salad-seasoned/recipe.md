# Seasoned Sausage and Tomato Salad (Ensalada de tomate con salchicha aliñada)

Serves 2. Written from the footage and the cook's own Spanish narration (transcribed), so amounts are as spoken, not measured.

## Ingredients
- Large tomatoes and cherry tomatoes
- Fresh pork sausages, white wine
- Dressing: garlic, onion, ginger (optional, not required), olive oil, lemon juice, honey, coarse salt, black pepper, oregano

## Steps as shown
1. Wash the tomatoes, slice the large ones thin and halve the cherry tomatoes; set aside.
2. Chop garlic and onion very fine, add them to a mortar.
3. Add a tiny piece of ginger, crushed (optional).
4. Add olive oil, lemon juice, honey, coarse salt and black pepper; whisk hard until it emulsifies.
5. Lay the tomato slices on a plate with the cherry tomatoes between; spoon the dressing over.
6. Skin and chop the sausage, brown it in a small pan with a little oil and a spoon of the dressing over high heat.
7. Add a splash of white wine and let it reduce.
8. Spoon the sausage over the tomatoes and pour the pan juices on top.
