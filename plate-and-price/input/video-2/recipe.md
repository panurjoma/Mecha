# Gnocchi al Blue Cheese and Veggies (Ñoquis al queso azul y verduras)

Serves 2. Cuisine: Italian-inspired. Written from the footage (head-mounted camera, no speech), so amounts are NOT stated.

## Ingredients seen
- Potato gnocchi (ready-made), cooked in boiling water
- Leek, onion, green pepper, cherry tomatoes
- Cooking cream (a carton), blue cheese, Gruyère (grated), black pepper, cooking oil and a little butter
- A terracotta cup is poured over the veg once (liquid not identified)

## Steps as shown
1. Wash and chop the leek, slice the onion, dice the green pepper.
2. Soften the green pepper, onion and leek in a pan with oil and butter; set the cooked veg aside on a plate.
3. Boil water for the gnocchi.
4. In a second pan warm the cream, add chunks of blue cheese and stir until melted; season with pepper; add grated cheese.
5. Return the cooked leek to the sauce; chop cherry tomatoes and add them.
6. Boil the gnocchi, drain them and tip them into the sauce with a little pasta water; mix.
7. Plate, grate Gruyère on top, finish.
