# Udon teriyaki with Iberian pork and prawns (Udon teriyaki con secreto ibérico y gambones)

Serves 2. Cuisine: Japanese-inspired. About 30 minutes.
Written by Claude from the frames of `raw.mp4` (the video has no speech, only music), so check amounts and steps.

## Ingredients seen in the video
- Fresh udon noodles (two bags)
- Secreto ibérico (Iberian pork shoulder cut), thin slices
- King prawns (gambones), shell on, with garlic
- Mushrooms (white button and shiitake), sliced
- Onion, carrots (sticks), garlic, spring onions
- Teriyaki sauce made in a bowl from: soy sauce (several bottles on the counter), Chinese rice wine, brown sugar, dark sauce
- Cooking oil, pepper or spice mix

## Steps (as shown)
1. Lay out and prep: slice mushrooms, onion, carrot; chop garlic.
2. Heat a pan with oil and soften the garlic.
3. Cook mushrooms, onion and carrot until browned.
4. Loosen the fresh udon in hot water and drain.
5. Mix the teriyaki sauce in a bowl (soy, rice wine, brown sugar, dark sauce).
6. Sear the king prawns on a plate-pan and set aside.
7. Griddle the secreto ibérico slices.
8. Add noodles to the veg, pour in the sauce, return pork and prawns, toss.
9. Plate with chopped spring onion.
