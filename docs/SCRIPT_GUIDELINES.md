# Cooking scripts, storytelling and YouTube review

Active channel: **The Edge Kitchen**. Updated 2026-10-23. This guide supersedes earlier physics/Shorts defaults, preserved in [Proposals](../Proposals/README.md).

## Mandatory entry point
Before every script, narration, storyboard, title, thumbnail or world/scene prompt:
1. Read the current repository's `AGENTS.md`, this guide, [channel brief](THE_EDGE_KITCHEN.md) and [assistant workflow](ASSISTANT_WORKFLOW.md). Use the active PR branch until merge, then the default branch. Record the resolved commit SHA in the editorial review.
2. Inspect current episode/asset revisions and creator feedback. Do not invent approvals or substitute an old chat summary for these files.
3. Apply the confirmed cooking direction. Proposals are historical research, not active instructions.
4. Check the relevant official YouTube policy when deciding a policy question; recheck before publication, on a new risk or known policy change, or if the last review is over 30 days old. That interval is our maintenance choice, not a YouTube rule.
5. Keep a companion editorial review with the episode handoff. The CLI validates structure, not policy compliance, culinary truth or monetization.

## Audience and voice
Spanish from Spain first; use accessible language with a natural formal/informal mix. Celebrate ingredient flavor, variety and relaxed island/surf life. Use the creator's own recorded voice. English localization is optional and must preserve recipe meaning, units, allergens and timing. No unapproved voice cloning or impersonation.

Show the dish and its appeal early. Explain useful choices through concrete actions: what to add, what to look for, why a step matters. Avoid long introductions, filler, forced slang and unsupported health or income promises. Match duration to the recipe; no Shorts or long-form duration is yet fixed.

## Recipe and visual truth
Record servings, measured ingredients, preparation order, relevant temperatures, timings, doneness cues, allergens and substitutions. Mark estimates and unresolved testing honestly. Verify safety-critical advice against appropriate primary food-safety guidance for the recipe; do not invent safe temperatures or substitute photorealistic images for testing.

Distinguish tested recipes, sourced adaptations and untested concepts. Do not claim a dish was cooked or tasted if only generated. Avoid guaranteed weight-loss, disease-treatment or nutrition claims without adequate evidence.

Track ingredient and food states across shots: raw/cooked, quantities, cut size, cookware, utensil positions and plating. Inspect hands, heat, steam, liquids and appliance behavior. Reject unsafe or physically implausible actions. The decorative cooking hearth does not establish real-world ventilation or combustion safety.

The floor plan governs the recurring kitchen. Check island angle, clear separation from sofa, corner glazing and appliance positions. Reference images with inconsistent geometry are not approved multiview evidence.

## Originality, rights and platform review
Create distinct recipes, useful instruction and original narration. Shared scenery alone does not make videos repetitive, but cosmetic prompt swaps over interchangeable substance are not a sound channel strategy. Research demand without copying another creator's script or distinctive presentation. A public transcript, credit or lack of a claim is not a reuse license.

Use cleared music, footage, graphics and voices; retain license/attribution records and provider commercial-use terms. Check the actual final video, audio, thumbnail, title and description.

Official policy references, to verify at the checkpoints above:
- [Altered/synthetic content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en): assess realistic generated scenes and audio using the current disclosure rules. Use Studio's required declaration when applicable; a description note is not a replacement. Do not conceal qualifying AI use.
- [Channel monetization](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en): review originality, repetitive/inauthentic and reused-content considerations.
- [Advertiser-friendly guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278?hl=en): consider the entire release package.
- [Community Guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9288567?hl=en): review relevant safety and deception risks.
- [Copyright](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797466?hl=en): asset permission and monetization eligibility are separate questions.

These links are review entry points, not a claim of a new publication clearance on 2026-10-23. Recheck live documents for the actual release. No topic, voice or wording guarantees approval or revenue. Validate demand and economics with measured evidence; treat tutorial revenue claims as unverified.

## Required companion review
Record episode/revision; repo SHA and guides consulted; recipe source/testing status; servings, safety and allergen checks; original angle; narration and localization; selected kitchen/world version; asset sources and rights; visual continuity checks; disclosure decision with source/check date; originality, advertiser suitability and Community Guidelines findings; creator corrections/approvals; actual pilot costs and next action.

Keep unresolved findings explicit. Do not add invented policy fields to the strict existing JSON schema. Save the companion review alongside the durable episode bundle; current exports do not automatically include arbitrary sidecars.
