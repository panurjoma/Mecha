# AI Cooking — planned YouTube channel

**Status:** Planned / documentation only (2026-10-23). **Name:** AI Cooking is a working label, not an approved public brand.

## Concept
An additional YouTube channel within the Mecha creator portfolio focused on **AI-generated cooking videos**. Videos should present original recipe concepts or clearly sourced adaptations, ingredients, preparation steps and appealing food-focused visuals. No specific cuisine, format, target audience or publishing cadence has yet been chosen.

## Editorial and production intentions
- Explore AI-generated visuals, animation, narration and recipe-storyboarding, while keeping the human creator in control of scripts, accuracy and publication.
- Make quantities, timing, preparation methods, food safety and final results coherent and practically usable. AI visuals must not imply that a real dish was prepared or tested when it was not.
- Verify recipe and food-safety claims where relevant. Review copyright/asset rights, synthetic-media disclosure and YouTube originality/monetization policies before publication.
- Prefer reusable Mecha concepts (source provenance, scripted scenes, asset tracking, review and revision history), but **do not assume the current landscape prototype can render cooking videos**.

## Decisions still open
- Final channel name, language and audience.
- Shorts vs long-form, recipe categories and tone.
- Visual approach (fully synthetic footage, stylized animation or a hybrid), voice, music and any production providers.
- First pilot recipe, review criteria, cost budget and publishing schedule.

## Scope of this change
This is **portfolio planning only**. It does not create a YouTube account, produce a video, authorize paid tools, implement a rendering pipeline or change the priorities of the existing Shorts tracks. It should be considered alongside `docs/SHORTS_STRATEGY_AUDIT.md` and the general review principles in `docs/SCRIPT_GUIDELINES.md`.
