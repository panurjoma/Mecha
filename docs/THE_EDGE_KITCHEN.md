## Fixed layout intent
The creator's hand-drawn floor plan is the layout authority. Read it with the handwriting upright:
- Ocean-facing glazing spans the upper wall and turns the upper-right corner. A round dining table sits by that corner.
- The central island is diagonal: hob toward the upper-left, preparation/cutting in the middle, sink toward the lower-right.
- Hob: four induction zones around one central gas burner. One sink and tap; no duplicate cooking or washing station on the wall counter.
- The left wall contains the utensil/work area, ovens and twin refrigerators. Keep the artwork and entrance consistent with the plan.
- The sofa faces the right solid wall, with a long low coffee table in between. Television and elevated glass-door cooking hearth are adjacent on that wall, not stacked.
- Preserve generous circulation, especially between island and sofa, and around the dining table. A roughly 1.3–1.5 m island-to-sofa clearance is a visual design target to check in a measured model, not an as-built measurement.

Warm walnut, ivory stone, bronze details, restrained amber lighting, discreet herbs and minimal decoration define the material palette. Include storage for pans, pots, utensils, spices and small appliances without filling every surface.

The earlier approximate room area is conceptual, not a dimensioned plan. The cooking hearth is a visual concept; indoor combustion, ventilation and gas arrangements require separate qualified design if ever built physically.
