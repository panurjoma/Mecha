# Mecha vision

Mecha supports original, researched, human-reviewed video production through conversation. **The Edge Kitchen is the first active YouTube channel**: enjoyable, varied cooking in a recognizable coastal setting.

Build one reproducible pilot before expanding the channel portfolio. Reuse scenery and production tooling while making every recipe and explanation substantive and distinct. Keep the creator in control of accuracy, style, budget and publication.

The assistant maintains durable episode history, asset provenance and explicit approvals. Automation should reduce repetitive production work without hiding uncertainty or claiming that synthetic footage proves a recipe works.

See the [channel brief](THE_EDGE_KITCHEN.md), [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and [deferred proposals](../Proposals/README.md). Earlier versions of the vision are preserved in that proposals directory.
