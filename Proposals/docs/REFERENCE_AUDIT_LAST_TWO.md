### Scientific quality flags
- The returned transcript switches from about 93 million MILES to 93 million KILOMETRES. NASA gives about 93 million miles / 150 million kilometres. This is a concrete transcript-level unit inconsistency; an ASR mistake versus spoken mistake has not been independently resolved.
- Precise cooling values and infrastructure/ecological timelines are asserted without linked calculations or references in the retrieved description. Treat as unsupported in this evidence set, not established predictions.

- A disappearing/reappearing Sun is an impossible setup. Any orbital demonstration must explicitly declare its toy-model assumptions; a dramatic visualization is not a validated simulation.
