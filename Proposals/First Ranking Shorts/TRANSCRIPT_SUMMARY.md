## Format extracted
A visible category explains the promise immediately. Each ranked segment supplies a small payoff while the remaining positions create anticipation. The final position resolves the main promise. The tutorial suggests around 5–7 clips; Mecha proposes testing 3–5 explained comparisons in 35–60 seconds, a separate hypothesis.

## What a creator still does
