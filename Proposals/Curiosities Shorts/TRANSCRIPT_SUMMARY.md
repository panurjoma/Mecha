## Narrative method
Begin with a concrete curiosity, give enough information to understand the puzzle, explain one mechanism and resolve it. Supporting shots follow the spoken idea. Avoid a long greeting and do not withhold the answer merely to force a loop. The tutorial's short-cut rhythm is a creative convention, not an established retention guarantee.

## Mecha differences
Accessible es-ES, own voice, primary-source checks, original explanatory diagrams, no default equations and honest disclosure. No disguised synthetic evidence, copied narration or unauthorized social footage. Full third-party transcript is intentionally not republished.
